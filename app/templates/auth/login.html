How to register as a member

  1. Open Member registration.
  2. Select your Global → Country → State → Group → District church.
  3. If your church is not listed yet, choose the Knowsoft church hierarchy so you can still join and use the app.
  4. Complete your profile and submit — your district admin approves access.
  5. After approval, sign in here and open Subscription if prompted.

Invite & earn (rewards)

  • Every member gets a unique promo code on the rewards page.
  • Share your code or join link. Friends register and take a paid subscription.
  • Track active subscribers on your code. At 50 active, cashout unlocks (request on the 30th with bank details and ID).
  • After you request payment, your counter starts again for new referrals only.
  • Referred members can receive 3 free months when their first paid plan is confirmed.
